Privacy Policy for StackCado

Last Updated: July 12, 2026

StackCado ("we", "our", or "us") is a Chrome extension that shows you the technology stack of the website you are visiting. This policy describes exactly what data the extension handles. The short version: detection runs entirely in your browser, and we do not collect your browsing data — or any usage analytics — at all.

1. What the Extension Processes Locally

  • On each page you visit, the extension reads the page's script URLs, cookie names (never values), and a small snippet of the page's HTML head, inside your browser only, to match them against a list of known technology signatures.
  • These results are kept in your browser's temporary extension storage and are discarded when the tab closes.
  • None of this information is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

2. Network Requests the Extension Makes

The extension makes only these requests, none of which include your browsing data:

  • Data files (about once per day): it downloads public detection signatures, pricing data, and configuration from our public GitHub repository (stackcado-data, served via GitHub Pages).
  • Licence verification (Pro only): if you buy StackCado Pro and enter a licence key, the extension sends only that key to our licence server to confirm it is valid — when you activate, and roughly once a week afterwards. No URLs, page content, or personal details are included.

3. StackCado Pro Purchases

  • Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details.
  • To let you recover a lost licence key, our licence server stores the email address you used at checkout together with your licence key. That is the only personal data we store, and it is used only to deliver and recover your key (recovery emails are sent via Resend).
  • To have this record deleted, email us and we will remove it (your licence stops working once deleted).

4. Scan History and Exports (Pro)

If you use "Save scan", the scan (site hostname and detected tools) is stored locally in your browser's extension storage so you can compare sites later. You can clear it at any time from the popup. Exports (CSV, JSON, report) are generated locally and saved only to your device. Your licence status syncs across your own browsers via your Chrome profile's extension sync storage.

5. What We Do NOT Do

  • No usage analytics or telemetry of any kind.
  • No collection of browsing history, URLs, cookies, or page content.
  • No selling, trading, or sharing of personal information.
  • No account or sign-up is required for any feature; Pro needs only a licence key.

6. Affiliate Disclosure

StackCado links to reviews on HighTicketDeals.com. Some of those reviews contain affiliate links, which is disclosed in the extension and on the store listing. Links open only when you click them — the extension never redirects you or modifies the pages you visit.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If our data handling ever changes materially, we will disclose it prominently in the extension before the change takes effect.
