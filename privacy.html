Privacy Policy for StackCado

Last Updated: May 28, 2026

StackCado ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the StackCado Chrome extension (the "Extension").

1. Information We Collect

To provide its core functionality, StackCado may process the following types of information:

  • Technical Information: When you visit a website, the Extension analyzes the site's technology stack (e.g., CMS, frameworks, analytics tools) locally in your browser.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous, aggregated data about how the Extension is used to improve our services. This does not include any personally identifiable information (PII).

2. How We Use Your Information

The information processed by StackCado is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To identify and display the technology stack of the websites you visit.
  • To provide cost-saving suggestions based on the detected technologies.
  • To improve the accuracy of our detection signatures.

3. Data Storage and Security

StackCado is designed with privacy in mind:

  • Local Processing: Most analysis happens locally on your device. We do not track your browsing history or store the URLs you visit on our servers.
  • No Personal Data Sale: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The Extension may fetch updated detection signatures and pricing data from our public GitHub repository (stackcado-data). This process does not involve sending any of your personal data to GitHub.

5. Compliance with Laws

We will disclose your information where required to do so by law or subpoena or if we believe that such action is necessary to comply with the law and the reasonable requests of law enforcement.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
